# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Potential clients use the site to review real websites, applications, and business systems, explore case studies and starting prices, and get in touch.
- Recruiters and collaborators use it to understand Favour’s practical capabilities, learning path, teamwork, and how to connect.
- Friends and curious visitors explore his life timeline, music, anime, competitive Call of Duty Mobile, and Arsenal memories.

## Product Purpose

IMEGU.WORLD is Favour Imegu’s (Gojo’s) personal, multi-page portfolio. It introduces the developer behind the work, shows what he builds and how he collaborates, and shares the experiences and interests that shape him. Success means visitors can evaluate the work and start a conversation when there is a fit.

## Positioning

This is a personal developer portfolio rather than a generic agency landing page. It connects practical web and software work with Favour’s real personal history and interests, so visitors can understand both the work and the person behind it.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the responsive website on desktop and mobile. Potential clients move between project case studies, services, and pricing, then contact Favour through WhatsApp, email, or the message form. Recruiters and collaborators review capabilities and working history. Friends and curious visitors explore personal stories and hobbies.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve Home, About, Work, Services, Hobbies, Pricing, Contact, and Ask; every individual project case study; project search and filtering, including the dust-disintegration effect; pricing and service content; deep links; mobile navigation; résumé download; form-to-WhatsApp/email workflow; theme toggle; Saka Easter egg; Arsenal atmosphere; and SEO.
- Keep the existing React/Vite implementation and project architecture intact during refinements.
- Never fabricate metrics, client testimonials, achievements, or other proof.
- Controls and core flows must work without hover or animation. Verify desktop and phone layouts.
- Work in reviewable batches on a feature branch. Publish only after explicit user approval.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name IMEGU.WORLD and identify the person as Favour Imegu (Gojo).
- The brand emphasizes red, charcoal, and near-white rather than burgundy.
- Personal writing sounds like a person, not a marketing agency, and keeps the distinctions among different hobbies.
- Acknowledge Professor Oyebade and retain the user’s authentic personal stories.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project case studies and live work, including Afri Index and BlessingFoundHerBlessing.
- The résumé, personal images, life stories, and hobby content in the site.
- The site must not invent customer testimonials, performance benchmarks, or achievement claims. Where evidence is absent, keep that absence clear.

## Product Principles

- Show real work and real experiences; never fabricate proof.
- Keep the writing personal and preserve the stories that explain the work.
- Preserve the existing routes, project journeys, and contact workflows when refining the interface.
- Keep the site and its controls usable on both phones and desktop.
