# IMEGU.WORLD — Product context

## What this site is
A personal, multi-page portfolio for Favour Imegu (Gojo). This is not a SaaS dashboard or a generic agency landing page. It lets visitors meet the developer behind the work: what he builds, how he collaborates, what he enjoys and the experiences that have shaped him.

## Visitors and their goals
- Potential clients: find evidence of real websites, applications and business systems; explore project case studies; understand starting prices and get in touch.
- Recruiters and collaborators: understand practical capabilities, the learning path, teamwork and how to connect.
- Friends and curious visitors: explore the life timeline, music, anime, competitive CODM and Arsenal memories.

## Architecture to preserve
Home, About, Work, Hobbies, Pricing and Contact; every individual existing project case study; current project search/filter (including the dust-disintegration effect), price/service content, deep links, mobile navigation, resume download, form-to-WhatsApp/email workflow, theme toggle, Saka Easter egg, Arsenal atmosphere and SEO.

## Personal principles
- Real personality, real projects and real experiences. Never fabricate metrics, client testimonials, or achievements.
- “Nobody is a bank of knowledge. We all need each other.” This connects learning to delegate at university to working alongside Dev Boyz.
- Acknowledge Professor Oyebade and retain the user's authentic personal stories.
- Personal writing should sound like a person, not a marketing agency. Maintain the distinctions among the different hobbies.
