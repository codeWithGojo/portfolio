# Portfolio assets

## Football photographs and artwork

- The supplied Bukayo Saka shirt photograph and 07 Starboy artwork were provided for this redesign. Their source files are preserved in [assets/source](assets/source). The 204 × 192 photograph is published at its original resolution and has not been upscaled. Photographer and graphic designer credits were not supplied.
- The contextual 2025 Bukayo Saka and Myles Lewis-Skelly photographs are both by Chensiyuan under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See the [Saka source file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1_bukayo_saka_arsenal_2025_(cropped).jpg) and [Lewis-Skelly source file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1_Myles_Lewis-Skelly_arsenal_2025_(cropped).jpg). The source files are cropped versions of the original images and disclose prior AI upscaling by their uploader. The portfolio versions were downsampled and encoded as WebP and remain under the same license. These photographs are contextual portraits from 2025. They do not depict the 2026 matches described in the stories.
- The three attached phone screenshots were reviewed as design references and are not shipped as site images.

## Typefaces

The site includes the Latin upright weight-axis WOFF2 files from [Fontsource Inter Variable 5.3.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/inter/v/5.3.0) and [Fontsource Space Grotesk Variable 5.3.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/space-grotesk/v/5.3.0). Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license texts accompany the distributed font files in `public/fonts`.

## Anime portraits

Favour supplied the five JPEGs used on the character cards: Satoru Gojo (225 × 225), Yuno (331 × 604), Yuta Okkotsu (447 × 447), Katsuki Bakugo (163 × 192), and Endeavor (150 × 300). The originals are served from `public/media`, and the cards display the artwork without cropping. Artist credits and independent license information were not supplied.
